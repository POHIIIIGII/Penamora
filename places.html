PALAWAN

El Nido

Turquoise lagoons, dramatic limestone cliffs, white-sand beaches, and breathtaking island scenery.

Discover El Nido

El Nido is a municipality in northern Palawan famous for its spectacular limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, white-sand beaches, and rich marine biodiversity.

The area is surrounded by numerous islands and islets, making it one of the Philippines' most popular destinations for island-hopping and other water activities.

PALAWAN

Coron

Crystal-clear lakes, towering limestone cliffs, beautiful beaches, and famous underwater wrecks.

Discover Coron

Coron is one of Palawan's most popular destinations. It is known for its clear waters, limestone formations, beautiful lakes, and spectacular diving locations.

Some of its famous attractions include Kayangan Lake, Barracuda Lake, Twin Lagoon, and the historic World War II shipwrecks found around Coron Bay.

IFUGAO

Banaue

Ancient rice terraces carved into the mountains of the Cordillera.

Discover the Banaue Rice Terraces

The Banaue Rice Terraces are among the Philippines' most recognizable cultural landscapes. The terraces demonstrate the agricultural knowledge and traditions of the Ifugao people.

The Rice Terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras are recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage property. The terraces are also an important part of Ifugao cultural heritage.

ILOCOS SUR

Vigan City

A historic city where Spanish-era architecture meets Filipino culture.

Discover Vigan City

Vigan is one of the Philippines' best-preserved historic cities. It is known for its distinctive architecture, cultural heritage, and historic streets.

Calle Crisologo is particularly famous for its cobblestone streets and historic houses, giving visitors a glimpse into Vigan's rich history.

Siquijor

A peaceful island known for waterfalls, beaches, marine life, and cultural traditions.

Discover Siquijor

Siquijor is an island province known for its beautiful beaches, waterfalls, marine biodiversity, and distinctive cultural heritage.

The island combines natural attractions with cultural experiences, making it an interesting destination for visitors looking for both adventure and relaxation.
